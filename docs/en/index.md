# Rafael Martínez Estévez

<p class="lead">Computer Science professor and researcher · Software engineer</p>

Assistant professor at the University of Pinar del Río «Hermanos Saíz Montes de Oca» (UPR). I work at the Center for Learning and Research Resources (CRAI), where I lead software projects for managing, monitoring and evaluating scientific information. I also administer the university's scientific journals and institutional repository.

[Projects](/en/proyectos) · [About](/en/sobre-mi) · [Publications](/en/publicaciones) · [Contact](/en/contacto) · [CV in English](../Resume-en-Rafael-Martinez-Estevez.pdf) · [CV en español](../Resume-es-Rafael-Martinez-Estevez.pdf)

## Areas of work

- **Research information systems (CRIS).** Design and development of platforms to record, monitor and evaluate scientific output.
- **Knowledge graphs and linked data.** Ontology-based modelling with Neo4j, together with hybrid retrieval on search engines such as Typesense and Elasticsearch.
- **Applied artificial intelligence.** Integration of language models into library services and experimentation with models such as multilingual BERT.
- **Infrastructure and open-source software.** Container-based deployment, automation, and administration of journal and repository systems.

## Projects

**Iroko-CRIS and Sceiba Platform** — Project lead, analyst and developer. Research information system for monitoring Cuban scientific publications, built with Neo4j, FastAPI and Angular. [Read more →](/en/proyectos#iroko-cris-and-sceiba)

**UPR-K** — Project lead, analyst and developer. Platform exploring knowledge graphs and language models to rethink university library services. [Read more →](/en/proyectos#upr-k)

**Tocororo** — Project coordinator and administrator of the GitHub organization that brings together CRAI-UPR software. [Repositories →](https://github.com/tocororo)
