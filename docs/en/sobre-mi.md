# About

<ProfilePhoto alt="Portrait of Rafael Martínez Estévez" />

Computer Science professor and researcher, and software engineer, with more than 15 years of experience in the design, construction and deployment of open-source data platforms.

Since 2016 I have worked at the University of Pinar del Río «Hermanos Saíz Montes de Oca» (UPR), where I serve as an assistant professor and technical lead, and develop scientific information platforms at the Center for Learning and Research Resources (CRAI).

My work combines teaching, applied research and systems development: from the architecture of a national CRIS to the administration of institutional repositories and scientific journals.
